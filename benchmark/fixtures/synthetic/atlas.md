# Atlas Vision Dataset

The Atlas Vision Dataset contains exactly 10,000 annotated wildlife images collected by
120 volunteer participants in 2021. Each image has one species label and one habitat label.
Atlas is released under the CC BY 4.0 license and supports image classification.

| Split | Images | Participants |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Train | 8,000 | 100 |
| Test | 2,000 | 20 |

The collection is English-language only. Known limitations include geographic imbalance and
under-representation of nocturnal species.
