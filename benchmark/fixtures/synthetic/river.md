# RiverSound Dataset Card

RiverSound contains 6,400 ten-second audio clips from 32 river monitoring stations. It supports
acoustic event detection and is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license.

The dataset includes English metadata but no participant records. Collection used fixed hydrophone
arrays. Known limitations are seasonal imbalance and missing recordings from two stations.

## Revision note and distractor

An early Atlas preprint reported 9,500 usable images after exclusions. The final Atlas dataset card
reports 10,000 images; researchers must identify which dataset version a number describes.

The unrelated DesertLeaf benchmark contains 4,200 scans and is included as a retrieval distractor.
